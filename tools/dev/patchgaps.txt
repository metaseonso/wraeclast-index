- The various endgame quests that lead to the Arbiter of Ash encounter have been consolidated into a single quest.
- Moved the location of the stash in Simulacrum to make it harder to accidentally start the encounter when trying to open the stash.
- The number of Monsters spawned from Breaches has been reduced.
- Strongboxes now spawn slightly more packs of Monsters in Maps.
- If you exit the game while in an endgame map area, logging back into the same character will now place you back inside the map instance if it still exists.

- Updated the Ice Crystal Keyword to clarify that it blocks movement of smaller monsters, while larger monsters will destroy any crystals in their path.

## 0.5.5 Patch Notes / Trial of Chaos Improvements
- A very frustrating point of the Trial of Chaos was that once you started a run, you had to complete that entire run before being able to leave, this meant if you needed to say trade, or stop playing the game for the night, you couldn't do so. Now you can leave the Trial of Chaos at any point and resume your run at a later time from the same room.
- There is now a reward chest at the end of each room in the Trial of Chaos. This makes dying less punitive as you don't lose your entire set of rewards.
- When completing an Inscribed Ultimatum with 10 Trials, you are now able to continue your run immediately without providing another Inscribed Ultimatum item. You're able to do this twice per Inscribed Ultimatum, and if successful, will be awarded a different Trialmaster Fragment for each subsequent successful run. The Modifiers and quality of rewards will be carried over when continuing a run, so you can choose to have better rewards and more difficulty.
- When given the option to continue your run, you are also offered an optional double or nothing wager of Currency, similar to the existing Magic Inscribed Ultimatums that could drop from the Trialmaster.
- The Uxmal, the Beastlord Boss Fight in the Trial of Chaos has had its arena redesigned entirely. It no longer needs to fly around being annoying.
- The more significant, elite Monsters that spawn during the Trial of Chaos are now more dangerous, but more rewarding.
- The Modifiers you select at the start of each encounter have been adjusted. In general we have added more Modifier Tiers, and significantly reduced the amount of difficulty they were applying.
- Turrets and Petrification Statues added by Modifiers have been made non-exclusive with each other. Previously if one of these Modifiers were chosen, the other would never appear after that point.
- The location that Turrets and Petrification Statues appear in all encounter rooms has been adjusted. In general they are more consistently spread out, and more fairly placed.
- The Petrification Statues Modifier has had its Tiers simplified. This Modifier starts off by adding 2 Petrification Statues to each encounter, and higher Tiers will increase the amount of Petrification Statues in each room.
- The Modifiers applied between rooms in the Trial of Chaos that add difficulty now always also increase the quality of drops from slain monsters.
- The Statue in the Escort the Statue encounter now has a short grace period when you are allowed outside its area of effect before it stops moving, preventing cases where you'd accidentally step out briefly and then have to wait for the statue to stop, return to the ground, then raise again before moving.
- The Statue in the Escort the Statue encounter has had its visuals updated to be more Vaal-themed, and the edge of the effect now has better clarity.
- The Trialmaster will now interrupt your Trial of Chaos less often with his timestopping abilities, now only stopping time at the very start of the entire run as opposed to in almost every single room. He will still appear to deliver his dialogue, but will just not stop time.
- The doors between each encounter which open based on your proximity now start opening sooner as you approach them, and you allow you to walk through them earlier. These changes should make progressing between rooms much smoother, especially when using characters with higher movement speeds.
- The elevators inside the Trial of Chaos are now more responsive once you step onto them. Previously you could run over them and if you were fast enough the elevator would ascend after you accidentally ran off it.
- The levers to call the elevator now make the elevators start their ascent or descent much faster.
- The elevators to move between rooms have been made significantly larger so you can fit more party members on them.
