Privacy Policy

Effective date: 29 September 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Wraeclast Index ("the Site", "we", "us") collects, uses and discloses information when you visit wraeclastindex.fyi. By using the Site, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Information We Collect

Usage information. When you use the Site, we count the pages viewed, the controls selected, which cards are opened, how many searches find nothing (a count only, never the words), the approximate position of selections on a page, the device type (phone, tablet or desktop), the country (as determined by our hosting provider) and the referring website. A page sends these once, when you leave it or switch away, and each count is stored as an anonymous data point in Cloudflare Workers Analytics Engine, with the day and hour it was sent. No data point carries an identifier: no IP address, no cookie, no account and nothing you typed. Counts made before 28 September 2026 are stored as daily totals in our own database. Neither is associated with any individual.

Information you submit. If you submit feedback through the Suggest feature, we collect the text you submit and the page from which it was submitted. If you open or copy a search on the Trade page, we record the search criteria.

Information we do not collect. We do not collect names, email addresses or other contact details from visitors, and we do not store IP addresses.

3. Cookies and Local Storage

The Site does not use cookies to track visitors. Certain preferences (such as keybindings, pinned cards, Trade page searches and currency watch lists) are saved in your browser's local storage. This information remains on your device and is not transmitted to us. You may remove it at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

4. Do Not Track

The Site honours Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals. When either signal is present, no usage information is collected.

5. How We Use Information

  • To operate, maintain and improve the Site.
  • To understand how the Site is used.
  • To display aggregated information, such as popular Trade page searches.
  • To review and respond to feedback.
  • To prevent abuse, including spam and excessive requests.

6. Abuse Prevention

To limit abuse, we temporarily store a one-way hashed value derived from your IP address. The hash uses a key that is replaced daily. Stored values expire after one hour and are deleted daily. These values cannot be used to identify you. Cloudflare also counts requests per hashed address for one minute, to stop floods of requests; that count is not stored.

7. Service Providers

The Site is hosted by Cloudflare, Inc., which processes requests and maintains technical records for security and performance purposes, and provides privacy-focused web analytics that do not use cookies. Cloudflare processes information in accordance with its own privacy policy: Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

8. Third-Party Links

The Site contains links to third-party websites, including the official Path of Exile trade site. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those websites. We encourage you to review their privacy policies.

9. Data Retention

Usage data points in Cloudflare Workers Analytics Engine are kept for three months, after which Cloudflare deletes them. The daily totals in our database from before 28 September 2026, and submitted feedback, are retained for as long as necessary to operate the Site. Abuse-prevention values expire after one hour and are deleted daily.

10. Accounts

The Site does not currently offer user accounts. If accounts are introduced, this policy will be updated to describe the information collected and how it may be deleted before that feature becomes available.

11. Children's Privacy

The Site is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes take effect when posted on this page, and the effective date above will be revised accordingly.

13. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy may be submitted only through the Suggest feature on the Site.
