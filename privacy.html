Privacy

Wraeclast Index is a free fan site for Path of Exile 2. This page says what it keeps when you visit, and what it doesn't. Last changed 19 September 2026.

What we count when you visit

  • Page views and clicks, as totals per day: which page, which button or link (by its label, never anything you type), roughly where on the page you clicked, your device type (phone, tablet or desktop), your country (from Cloudflare), and how you arrived (the name of the site that linked you, or "direct").
  • No cookies for visitors, no account needed, and your internet address is not stored.
  • If your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, nothing is counted at all.

Stopping spam

To stop floods of notes or searches, the site keeps a scrambled form of your internet address for up to an hour. The scramble uses a secret that changes every day and is then thrown away, so it can't be turned back into your address.

Things you send us

  • Suggest notes: the text you write and the page you were on. No name, no email, no address.
  • Trade searches: when you open or copy a search on the Trade page, the search itself is counted so popular searches can be shown. Nothing about you is kept with it.

Kept only in your browser

Your keybindings, your Trade page search and your currency watch list are saved in your own browser (local storage). They are never sent to us. Clearing your browser's site data removes them.

Our hosting

The site runs on Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare handles your requests and keeps its own short-term records for speed and security, and its cookie-free Web Analytics counts visits and page speed. See Cloudflare's privacy policy. A backup copy of the site lives on GitHub Pages.

Links to other sites

Buttons that open the official trade site, poe.ninja builds, build planners or guides take you to those sites. Their own privacy rules apply there.

Accounts

There are no accounts yet. When sign-in arrives, this page will list exactly what an account keeps (for example your name, a scrambled password or your Google or Path of Exile account id, and your pins), and you will be able to delete your account and everything with it.
